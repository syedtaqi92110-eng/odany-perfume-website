The Spirit of Karrar

An Homage to Nocturnal Grandeur & Unyielding Grace

The name Karrar evokes an ancient archetype of steadfast courage—an unwavering presence that re-engages and conquers adversity with chivalrous dignity. Composed between our historical sanctuary in Grasse and the wild, untamed oud forests of the Far East, this fragrance was conceived for those who cherish presence without shouting.

At its epicenter is our proprietary 25-Year Matured Cambodian Oud, sourced from certified sustainable family groves that preserve wild Aquilaria trees. Every single drop macerates for a minimum of 180 days in temperature-shielded copper vats, allowing the raw animalic fervor of pure agarwood to blossom into an intoxicating, velvety nectar of smoked suede and salted caramel woods.

Extraction Hydro-Distillation Low flame copper alembics
Aging 180 Solar Days Cold cellar maceration
Classification Extrait Pur 32% concentrated oil
