## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an architectural, editorial digital flagship for an artisanal luxury perfume house. The visual language evokes tactile sensory memory: aged amber, hand-cast glass, cold stone, and golden extracts. 

The aesthetic marries classical editorial print layout (evoking French art journals and high-fashion ateliers) with razor-sharp modernist restraint. Visual rhythm relies on sweeping margins, strict structural alignment, delicate hairlines, and silent luxury. 

Avoid synthetic micro-interactions, heavy drop shadows, flashy skeuomorphism, and generic e-commerce visual noise. Every transition must move with deliberate poise—slow ease curves, soft atmospheric dissolves, and disciplined negative space.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from raw natural perfumery components—distilled agarwood, golden resins, and fibrous handmade paper.

- **Background System:** Canvas base is pristine absolute white (`#FFFFFF`), with tiered sections alternating into a delicate parchment ivory (`#F8F6F1`) to indicate material transitions (such as olfactive pyramids, discovery sets, or editorial notes).
- **Primary & Tonal Espresso:** `#3B241B` acts as the primary anchor for dominant interactive states, key headlines, and structural rules, while `#241710` provides foundational depth for deep typographic contrasts and solid navigation caps.
- **Accents:** Champagne gold (`#B69A67`) is preserved strictly for precious moments—award seals, subtle badge highlights, selected state indicators, or delicate ornamental dividers.
- **Text & Delimiters:** Primary reading typography uses soft obsidian `#292521` for warmth over pure black; secondary meta-text utilizes stone umber `#77716B`. All structural rules and separation lines strictly employ `#EAE5DE`.

## Typography

The typographic tension pairs the classical literary cadence of **EB Garamond** with the surgical precision and architectural airiness of **Manrope**.

- **Editorial Hierarchy:** All product names, poetic introductions, and chapter designations are set in EB Garamond at standard or relaxed letter spacing. Italic cuts may be utilized sparingly for fragrance concentration distinctions (e.g., *Extrait de Parfum*, *Nectar Botanique*).
- **Technical & Utility Information:** Olfactive compositions (Top, Heart, Base notes), price specifications, navigational triggers, volume markers (e.g., 100 ML — 3.4 FL. OZ.), and legal attributions use Manrope.
- **Casing & Rhythm:** Category headers, sub-labels, and CTAs adopt uppercase typography (`label-caps`) with deliberate wide kerning (0.18em) to evoke haute couture branding standards.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored in an uncrowded 12-column architectural grid on desktop, scaling to 6 columns on tablet and 2 columns on mobile. 

- **Outer Margins:** Expansive outer boundaries of `4rem` (64px) on desktop create an intentional gallery matting effect around the imagery and typography. Mobile adjusts to `1.5rem` (24px).
- **Proportional Pacing:** Sections breathe deeply; vertical stack spacing between editorial features employs `space-xl` (80px) to allow individual imagery and copy to hold solitary focus.
- **Rhythm Rules:** Never crowd perfume flacons or narrative excerpts. Product listings alternate between regular grid placements and offset, full-bleed storytelling banners to avoid cold commercial repetition.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated via delicate tone shifts and whisper-thin hairline boundaries rather than dramatic shadows.

- **Layering & Tone:** Elevating panels, sensory modal overlays, and fly-out cart drawers use the warm ivory tone (`#F8F6F1`) or absolute white (`#FFFFFF`) with 1px hairline borders (`#EAE5DE`).
- **Ambient Shadow (Reserved):** Floating sticky navigation bars or quick-view specimen trays receive an ultra-diffused, ambient shadow: `0 16px 36px -12px rgba(36, 23, 16, 0.04)`.
- **Atmospheric Veil:** Background overlays behind modals or mobile menus utilize a muted warm veil of `#241710` rendered at 35% opacity with an intentional `4px` blur backdrop filter to preserve ambient luxury without harsh detachment.

## Shapes

The design system is strictly **sharp-edged** (`roundedness: 0`). 

- Corners on all elements—including buttons, input containers, product cards, badges, and modals—remain clean 90-degree right angles.
- This geometric clarity mimics the physical edges of fine paper stationery, boutique perfume packaging, and crystal glass bases.
- Subtle inner borders or framed vignettes must always reinforce strict linear geometry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#3B241B` fill with `#FFFFFF` text. Minimal height (48px), 0px border radius, text styled in `label-caps`. Hover transition gently shifts the background to `#241710` alongside a subtle 1px border highlight in `#B69A67` over 350ms.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Transparent fill, 1px solid border in `#3B241B` or `#EAE5DE`, text in `#292521`. Hover state fills with warm ivory `#F8F6F1`.
- **Text Link CTA:** Bare text in `label-caps` followed by an extended en-dash or thin arrow, underlined with an offset hairline that expands from 0% to 100% width on hover.

### Form Inputs
- Single 1px bottom border (`#EAE5DE`) or clean four-sided wireframe box with transparent background.
- Floating placeholder labels transition smoothly into `label-caps` in `#77716B`. Focus state transitions the bottom border cleanly to `#3B241B`. Error states are indicated via a muted brick red hairline, accompanied by understated italicized EB Garamond helper text.

### Product & Editorial Cards
- Flat presentation on `#FFFFFF` or `#F8F6F1` background framed by an optional 1px `#EAE5DE` divider.
- Imagery maintains an editorial 3:4 portrait aspect ratio with soft, light-filled photography.
- Card metadata is centered: Fragrance title in `headline-sm`, concentration classification in italicized `body-sm`, and pricing in clean `price-lg`. Hover initiates an effortless crossfade to reveal the artisan bottle in context or its raw ingredients.

### Chips & Olfactory Tags
- Sharp, rectangular containers with 1px border in `#EAE5DE`.
- Text styled in `label-caps` using `#77716B`. Active or filtered state turns border to `#B69A67` with `#241710` typography.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square 14px indicators with a 1px `#3B241B` stroke. Selected checkboxes display a solid, delicate inner square in `#3B241B` leaving a 2px interior white margin. No rounded pill toggles.

### Fragrance Pyramid (Custom Domain Component)
- A tiered, three-row architectural layout detailing Top, Heart, and Base notes. Separated by fine horizontal rules (`#EAE5DE`), with note categories rendered in `label-caps` (`#B69A67`) and ingredients listed in EB Garamond (`body-lg`).
